## Two websites, two real people and my favorite way of learning

Before Fritzo, I started experimenting with web development by building two simple websites for people close to me.

My mum runs a cleaning business, and my brother has a children’s playroom. They both needed a clearer online presence, so I used the opportunity to build something real while learning.

I made both websites for free, on purpose.
That has always been the easiest way for me to learn something new. I would rather solve a real problem for a real person than build another imaginary coffee shop from a tutorial.

I have also noticed a growing demand for simple, well presented websites among small local businesses where I live. So this started as practice, but it may eventually become another direction I explore more seriously.

The websites are not huge technical projects. They were my first practical steps into web development, and a reminder that the things I begin out of curiosity often grow into something much bigger later.
